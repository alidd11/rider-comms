1. Riding safety comes first

  • Rider Comms is a companion for riding. It is not a substitute for your own attention, judgment, training, or the rules of the road.
  • Don't look at or touch the app while moving. Stop somewhere safe before using visual or touch controls. The app locks distracting controls when it detects sustained movement. That lock is an aid, not a guarantee.
  • Use only hands-free, legally permitted equipment (such as a helmet intercom) and follow local laws on device use while riding.
  • Navigation, hazard reports and nearby-rider information can be wrong, late or missing. Routes may be unsuitable or unsafe. Reports come from other riders and aren't verified. Always follow road signs, conditions and the law.
  • Don't use Rider Comms to coordinate illegal riding, racing, or evading enforcement. Police and speed camera reports are there to help riders slow down and ride safely; don't use them to avoid being stopped for an offence, or anywhere reporting them is against local law.

  • Give accurate information, and don't impersonate anyone.
  • You can delete your account at any time in Settings.
