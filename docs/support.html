Email support email to be confirmed and we'll reply within 2 working days. Safety reports made in the app are reviewed within 24 hours.

Common questions

I can't sign in. Use Forgot password? on the sign-in screen. We'll email a reset link and code to the address on your account.

I didn't get the verification email. Check your spam folder, then use Resend verification email in Settings → Account.

Someone is bothering me. Report and block them from their profile, a chat, the ride roster, a friend request or the Nearby Voice rider list. See the Community Guidelines for what happens next.

Voice isn't connecting. Check that Rider Comms can use your microphone (iPhone Settings → Rider Comms) and that you have a data connection. Your map and ride keep working while voice reconnects.

My location isn't showing. Location is only shared when you turn it on: Go live for Nearby, or the location switch in a group ride. Check that location access is allowed for Rider Comms.

Delete your account

In the app, or on the web after signing in: Settings → Account → Account and data → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, profile, messages, friendships, rides and reports straight away. Encrypted backups are kept for up to 30 days, then expire.

If you can't sign in (for example, your account is suspended), email support email to be confirmed from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you.
