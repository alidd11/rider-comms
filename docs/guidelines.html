Community Guidelines

How we expect riders to treat each other, and what happens when someone doesn’t.

Last updated 1 October 2026

On this page

  1. Zero tolerance
  2. Ride safely
  3. Report and block
  4. What happens after a report

Rider Comms connects riders who share the road. These guidelines apply everywhere in the app: voice, Nearby, group rides, messages, profiles, hideouts and hazard reports. Breaking them can get content removed and accounts suspended. They're part of our Terms of Service.

Zero tolerance

There is no place on Rider Comms for:

  • harassment, bullying, threats or intimidation, on voice or in text;
  • hate speech or slurs targeting race, ethnicity, religion, disability, gender, sexual orientation or any other protected characteristic;
  • sexual content, and anything that sexualises minors (we report this to the authorities);
  • stalking, or using location, ride or Nearby features to follow or locate someone who doesn't want to be found;
  • sharing anyone's location or personal information without their permission;
  • promoting violence, self-harm, illegal riding, racing on public roads or evading enforcement;
  • impersonation, spam, scams and unsolicited promotion;
  • false hazard or safety reports.

Display names, handles and other text are checked automatically, and text containing slurs or explicit language is rejected. That check is a floor, not a guarantee, which is why reporting matters.

Ride safely

  • Don't look at or touch your phone while moving. Rider Comms locks distracting controls when it detects you're riding.
  • Use hands-free voice only, with equipment that's legal where you ride.
  • Hazard reports and routes can be wrong. The road, signs and the law always come first.

Report and block

  • Report a rider from their profile, a chat, the ride roster, a friend request or the Nearby Voice rider list. Reports are confidential.
  • Block a rider from the same places. You'll stop hearing and seeing each other in Nearby, and they can't message you or send friend requests. They aren't told. You can unblock in Settings → Privacy → Blocked riders.
  • Mute a rider in Nearby Voice to stop hearing each other for the rest of the session.

What happens after a report

Our moderators review reports within 24 hours. We may remove content, restrict features, or suspend accounts, straight away where someone's safety is at risk. Suspended riders are signed out everywhere. If you think we got it wrong, contact support email to be confirmed and someone who wasn't involved will review the decision.

If someone is in immediate danger, contact the emergency services first.
