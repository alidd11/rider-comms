Rider Comms ("we", "us") operates Rider Comms, an app for motorcycle and moped riders that provides group voice, private group rides, rider presence nearby, hazard reports, messaging and navigation. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it and your choices.

We don't use advertising SDKs, don't sell personal data, don't track you across other apps or websites, and don't collect location in the background except during navigation you start. Your position is shared with other riders only while the app is open and sharing is on.

Cookies and storage on your device

Rider Comms doesn't set cookies, and uses no analytics or advertising cookies or trackers. The apps keep a small amount of data on your device only so they work:

  • Your sign-in, if you tick "Remember me": in the device's secure storage in the iOS and Android apps, or in the web app's local storage. Without it, the web app forgets your sign-in when you close the tab and the apps when they're closed.
  • Preferences: settings such as distance units, navigation app and Ride Safe, your recent place searches and your saved Home and Work.

This storage is strictly necessary for features you ask for, so it doesn't need a consent banner. You can clear it by signing out, clearing the app's data, or clearing your browser's site data.

The web app loads Google Maps directly in your browser to show the map. Google may use its own cookies or storage for that, under Google's privacy policy.

How long we keep data

Summary of our retention schedule:

  • Live locations are shown for about 30 seconds and deleted within an hour.
  • Your last nearby-sharing position is kept for 30 minutes, only to check that each new position is physically possible (this stops people faking locations to find other riders). It is never shown to anyone.
  • Hazard reports expire after 1–8 hours depending on type.
  • Ride join codes expire after 12 hours. Rides are deleted when the host ends them, or 30 days after creation at the latest.
  • Push notification addresses are kept until you turn notifications off on that device, sign out on it, or the push service tells us the address no longer works.
  • Sign-in sessions last 30 days. Email-verification links last 24 hours, and password-reset links last 1 hour.
  • Messages, friendships, profile and account data, and subscription details, are kept until you delete your account.
  • Reports and moderation decisions about a rider are kept until that rider's account is deleted.
  • Backups are kept for 30 days, so deleted data can remain in a backup for up to 30 days after deletion.
  • Server logs are kept for up to 30 days.

Your choices and rights

  • Location: sharing is off by default. Nearby sharing and each private ride's sharing are separate switches, and you can turn them off at any time. You can also withdraw location permission in your device settings.
  • Social handles: each can be Public, Friends only or Private.
  • Blocking and reporting are available from profiles, chats, ride rosters, friend requests and Nearby Voice. You can review and undo blocks in Settings.
  • Delete your account in Settings. This permanently deletes your account and associated data straight away (except backups, as above). If your account is suspended and you can't sign in, contact support email to be confirmed to request deletion.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, restrict or object to processing of your data, and to complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office). Contact support email to be confirmed and we'll respond within one month.

Safety and moderation

Reports are reviewed by our moderators. We may suspend accounts that break our Terms and Community Guidelines. We review reports within 24 hours. A suspension revokes all sign-ins. Decisions are logged, and you can appeal by contacting support email to be confirmed.

Security

Passwords and session tokens are stored only as one-way hashes. Traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Backups are encrypted. Access to production systems is limited to authorised staff who need it. No system is perfectly secure; we'll notify you and regulators of a breach where the law requires.

Children

Rider Comms is not intended for anyone under 16. We don't knowingly collect data from children. Contact us if you believe a child has created an account.

Changes

We'll update this policy when our practices change, and give notice in the app of material changes.
