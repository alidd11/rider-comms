# Rider Comms Support

Email **[support email]** and we'll reply within [2 working days]. Safety
reports made in the app are reviewed within 24 hours.

In danger or hurt? Call the emergency services first. Rider Comms can't send
help.

## Common questions

**I can't sign in.** Use *Forgot password?* on the sign-in screen. We'll
email a reset link and code to the address on your account.

**I didn't get the verification email.** Check your spam folder, then use
*Resend verification email* in Settings → Account and login → Email.

**Someone is bothering me.** Report and block them from their profile, a
chat, the ride roster, a friend request or the Nearby Voice rider list. See
the [Community Guidelines](guidelines.html) for what happens next. Reports
are reviewed within 24 hours.

**I'm not getting notifications.** Open Settings → Notifications and turn on
*Notifications on this device*, then check Rider Comms is allowed to send
notifications in your phone's settings. On iPhone, add the web app to your
Home Screen first.

**Voice isn't connecting.** Check that Rider Comms can use your microphone
(iPhone Settings → Rider Comms) and that you have a data connection. Your map
and ride keep working while voice reconnects.

**My location isn't showing.** Location is only shared when you turn it on:
*Go live* for Nearby, or the location switch in a group ride. Check that
location access is allowed for Rider Comms.

## Delete your account

In the app, or [on the web](https://alidd11.github.io/rider-comms/) after
signing in: **Settings → Account and login → Delete account**. This
permanently deletes your account, profile, messages, friendships, rides and
reports straight away. Encrypted backups are kept for up to 30 days, then
expire.

If you can't sign in (for example, your account is suspended), email
[support email] from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you.
