- Rider Comms is a companion for riding. It is **not** a substitute for your
  own attention, judgment, training, or the rules of the road.
- **Don't look at or touch the app while moving.** Stop somewhere safe before

  detects sustained movement. That lock is an aid, not a guarantee.
- Use only hands-free, legally permitted equipment (such as a helmet
  intercom) and follow local laws on device use while riding.
- **Navigation, hazard reports and nearby-rider information can be wrong,
  late or missing.** Routes may be unsuitable or unsafe. Reports come from
  other riders and aren't verified. Always follow road signs, conditions and
  the law.
